# Clustering Report (Task C, Milestone 1)

All numbers are regenerated by `python main.py cluster --algorithm kmeans [--compare]` (seed 42). Tables and figures are in `outputs/Clustering/`.

## 1. Data and preprocessing (done by the preprocessing member)

`kmeans_cleaned_scaled.csv` has **78,938 rows and 55 columns**, with no missing values. It is built from the 79,330-row `H2.csv` as follows:

| Step | Rows | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| H2.csv | 79,330 | 4 missing `Children` filled with 0 |
| Remove bookings with 0 adults | -390 | not a normal customer booking |
| Remove 2 bookings with 9 and 10 babies | -2 | implausible values |
| Final matrix | 78,938 | |

| Block | Columns | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Numeric | LeadTime, Adults, Children, Babies, TotalOfSpecialRequests, StaysInWeekendNights, StaysInWeekNights, ArrivalDateDayOfMonth | StandardScaler |
| Cyclic | Month_sin, Month_cos | sine/cosine of the arrival month |
| One-hot (45) | Meal, MarketSegment, DistributionChannel, ReservedRoomType, CustomerType, Country (top 15 + Other) | 0/1, unscaled |

`ReservedRoomType_P` is all zeros (no bookings), so it is dropped, leaving **54 features**. Each one-hot group sums to 1 on every row (tested).

**Row alignment.** The CSV has no row id. `src/clustering/data.py` rebuilds the same row filter from `H2.csv` and checks that scaling the rebuilt numeric columns reproduces the CSV (`verify_alignment`). This gives unscaled values for interpretation without trusting the row order.

**Split strategy.** Clustering is unsupervised, so there is no target, train/test split or test set. The scaler is fitted on all rows. No label is used to choose any parameter. In Milestone 2 stability will be checked by re-clustering random halves (adjusted Rand index).

## 2. Feature choice and leakage audit (clustering)

**Analysis point: booking creation time.** The segments describe who books, when and how, using only what is known at booking.

| Used | Why it is safe at the analysis point |
|---|---|
| LeadTime, Stays*Nights, Adults, Children, Babies | Stated on the booking |
| TotalOfSpecialRequests | Counted in the booking record. It can grow after booking, so it is the weakest of the used features; test dropping it in Milestone 2 |
| Meal, MarketSegment, DistributionChannel, ReservedRoomType, CustomerType, Country | Chosen at booking |
| Month_sin/cos, ArrivalDateDayOfMonth | Known arrival date |

| Excluded | Risk |
|---|---|
| IsCanceled, ReservationStatus, ReservationStatusDate | Direct outcome. Clusters would just separate canceled from not canceled |
| ADR | Pricing outcome and the regression target. Used only to describe clusters afterwards |
| AssignedRoomType | Set at or after check-in. It can differ from the reserved room because of overbooking, which is linked to cancellations |
| BookingChanges, DaysInWaitingList | Accumulate after booking, so they use future information |
| ArrivalDateYear, ArrivalDateWeekNumber | Year is a non-repeating time index. Week duplicates month |
| Agent, Company, DepositType, IsRepeatedGuest, Previous*, RequiredCarParkingSpaces | Near-constant (documented in the notebook). DepositType (Non Refund) is also strongly tied to the cancellation outcome |

**Indirect leakage.** `profile()` adds the cancel rate and mean ADR per cluster for interpretation only (columns prefixed `posthoc_`). They are never given to the algorithm and never used for choosing k. `load_scaled()` raises an error if any forbidden column enters the matrix (tested).

**Fitting order.** Preprocessing (encoding, scaling) happens once, before clustering, with no labels. There is no supervised test set in this task.

## 3. Baseline experiment (untuned)

K-Means on the full 78,938 rows with sklearn defaults: k = 8, k-means++, n_init = 10, seed 42. Silhouette uses a fixed 10,000-row sample (O(n²)). Davies-Bouldin and Calinski-Harabasz use all rows. Fit time 6 s.

| Silhouette | Davies-Bouldin | Calinski-Harabasz | Cluster sizes |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0.118 | 1.887 | 7,634 | 364 to 18,784 |

k sweep, k = 2 to 12 (`kmeans_k_sweep_v1.csv`, `kmeans_k_selection_v1.png`): silhouette stays between 0.09 and 0.12, Davies-Bouldin is best near k = 9 to 10 (1.86), and the elbow is gradual. **There is no sharp natural k, and cluster separation is weak.** This is common for mixed binary and numeric booking data. The final k is chosen in Milestone 2, not from this sweep.

PCA: PC1 and PC2 explain only 24% of the variance, and 11, 15 and 20 components are needed for 80%, 90% and 95%. The 2-D plot therefore overlaps heavily.

## 5. Problems found and what to change

1. **Babies cluster is a scaling artefact.** The 364 bookings with babies form their own cluster at every k of 4 or more. Babies is z-scored (z = 14 to 29), so it dominates the distance. Fix: use binary `HasChildren` and `HasBabies`.
2. **Skew.** Skewness: Babies 15, Children 4.5, StaysInWeekNights 2.5, LeadTime 1.4. Fix: log1p on LeadTime and the stay columns.
3. **Duplicates.** H2 has 25,902 exact duplicate rows. Classification and regression remove them, but the clustering matrix keeps them (53,053 unique of 78,938 rows). This needs one team-wide decision. Duplicates may be real group bookings, but they weight those segments by count. Removing them lowers the baseline silhouette from 0.118 to 0.095.
4. **ArrivalDateDayOfMonth** is a z-scored day number (1 to 31) with no booking-behaviour meaning. Test dropping it.
5. **Country** adds 16 sparse dimensions.

`--compare` runs the same untuned K-Means on four representations:

| Representation | Rows | Features | Silhouette | Davies-Bouldin | Smallest cluster |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v1: team matrix | 78,938 | 54 | 0.118 | 1.887 | 364 |
| v1_dedup: v1 without exact duplicates | 53,053 | 54 | 0.095 | 1.933 | 364 |
| v2: kid flags, log1p, no day of month | 78,938 | 53 | 0.104 | 2.070 | 4,336 |
| v2_no_country | 78,938 | 37 | 0.137 | 1.995 | 4,199 |

Silhouette values across different feature spaces are only indicative. v2 removes the babies cluster, and its clusters are more informative (for example a cluster with a 3-day lead time and a 12% cancel rate, and one with a 213-day lead time and a 70% cancel rate). Dropping Country raises silhouette but loses geographic information, so Country grouping (Portugal / rest of Europe / other) should be tested instead. **v1 stays the Milestone 1 baseline; the representation decision is a Milestone 2 task.**

## 6. Plan for Milestone 2

- Decide the representation (section 5) and the duplicate policy together with the other two sub-teams.
- Algorithms: K-Means (k = 2 to 15), Agglomerative (Ward, average, complete, on a documented stratified sample, with dendrogram), GMM (components and covariance type, BIC and silhouette), DBSCAN (eps from a k-distance plot). Early DBSCAN probes in the 54-D v1 space gave 86% noise at the default eps = 0.5, and 2 clusters at eps >= 3, so DBSCAN needs a better representation.
- Optimisation criterion: silhouette, then Davies-Bouldin, with BIC for GMM. No supervised labels. Baseline vs optimised table for every algorithm.
- PCA 2-D visualisation, stability check, final interpretation, saved artifacts, `cluster` CLI options and the app page.
